# Share card layout research — 2026-09-10

The user requested a complete layout rebuild and cited the many polished Solana cards. Reflective material is allowed; it must support readable identity and evidence. This research used agent-reach's Exa search and Jina public-page reader, then inspected the actual official Phantom sharing image in a browser. Third-party artwork is reference only and is not shipped.

## Primary references

- [Phantom: Perps updates](https://phantom.com/learn/blog/phantom-perps-updates). The official “Share PNL cards” image presents one large outcome, a compact entry/exit receipt, and recognizable authorship. Its hierarchy survives an X embed. Forecast borrows the immediate statement and compact supporting receipt, not monetary PnL, flames, percentages without denominators, or Phantom branding. Captured reference: `tmp/card-reference-phantom-pnl.png`.
- [Jupiter: Positions](https://docs.jup.ag/user-docs/trade/spot/positions) and [Portfolio](https://docs.jup.ag/user-docs/manage/portfolio/using-portfolio). The official documentation connects shareable summaries with win rate, realized/unrealized breakdowns and history. Inference: a share image should preserve the interpretation of a number, particularly its denominator and the difference between pending and final data. Forecast keeps correct/scored counts, invalid exclusions and provisional status.
- [Solana Mobile: .skr domain](https://docs.solanamobile.com/solana-mobile-stack/skr-domain). `.skr` names are readable identities in place of base58 addresses. Forecast's existing display name, including `spritz.skr`, leads the composition. A typed nickname is never presented as ownership verification.
- [Solana Mobile: Genesis Token](https://docs.solanamobile.com/solana-mobile-stack/seeker-genesis-token). Device ownership requires a separate verified token. No Seeker, chain-verified, NFT, rarity or token ownership badge is added by this redesign.
- [Seed Vault Wallet](https://solanamobile.com/blog/seed-vault-wallet-solana-seekers-native-mobile-wallet). The official surface combines dark fields, iridescent accents and readable identity. Material can signal ecosystem familiarity without borrowing a logo or inventing a verification claim.
- [Sora, Google Fonts source and OFL](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sora). Sora's broad geometric forms make short readable identities feel intentionally lettered. The variable font is self-hosted with its OFL license. System CJK glyph fallback remains explicit.

## Composition decision

Unique mechanism: a memorable identity carries a public, dated record whose strongest claim is supported by immutable finalized evidence. Scene: the card appears inside an X feed or a phone share sheet, often at one-third export size. Mode: Experience; the artifact leads, while the export panel remains an operating surface.

The discarded patterns are the settings-panel export and its predictable opposite, a giant profit percentage on a neon trading card. Seven grounded systems were considered: tournament result sheet; photographic contact strip; conference identity credential; split-flap arrival record; scientific field specimen label; sealed collector proof sleeve; anodized identity folio. Direction seed `81db7092` assigned candidate 7. The brief authorizes autonomous selection rather than a user decision board.

The selected folio puts identity first, then a large actual standout question or an honest beginning, with performance in a compact right-hand receipt. A folded iridescent plane is the material signature; it is original geometry, not a chart, fake badge, avatar, token, or reward. Portrait rearranges the evidence before a horizontal measurement receipt. Forecast cards share the folded material and strong typography, but the question leads and three independent probabilities remain distinct.

Catalog challengers were weighed on audience identification and product clarity: cutting-bench rails add sequence complexity to a small record; algorave syntax falsely suggests a technical instrument; forging/ticket/film-poster systems overstate physical claims or crowd actual evidence. None beats the identity folio on both axes. No reference composition or artwork is copied.

## Truth and export contract

The existing profile snapshot schema/hash, owner checks, captions, PNG/native sharing and two fixed profile sizes remain unchanged. Empty profiles have no performance scores, medals or invented history; samples under ten retain the explicit provisional line. Only supplied finalized history is drawn. Display-name text is never a device/domain verification badge. Points and wallet secrets remain absent. Synthetic preview fixtures are watermarked DEMO DATA by the verification harness, not stored as production data.

Verification is bounded to two batched visual rounds: four locales, both profile formats/materials, established/provisional/empty/extreme cases, forecast cards with missing/zero values, fonts loaded before rendering, and PNG export at native dimensions. The root agent independently reviews the final contact sheet.
